GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA
Department of Commerce and Industries
Notification No. CI/2024/089

Subject: Revised MSME Registration and Incentive Scheme - 2024

Date: 20th February 2024

This notification supersedes Notification No. CI/2023/045 dated 5th April 2023.

ELIGIBILITY FOR MSME BENEFITS:
All Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises registered in Karnataka with investment up to Rs. 50 crore and turnover up to Rs. 250 crore are eligible for incentives under the Karnataka Industrial Policy 2024.

KEY CHANGES:
1. Investment limit increased from Rs. 25 crore to Rs. 50 crore
2. Turnover limit increased from Rs. 100 crore to Rs. 250 crore
3. Registration fee waived for women entrepreneurs
4. Subsidy on electricity increased from 30% to 50% for first 3 years
5. Application deadline: 30th June 2024

INCENTIVES OFFERED:
- Capital subsidy: 15% of fixed capital investment (max Rs. 50 lakh)
- Interest subsidy: 5% on term loans for 5 years
- Electricity subsidy: 50% for first 3 years
- Land allotment at concessional rates
- Stamp duty exemption on land purchase

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Udyam Registration Certificate
- Project report with investment details
- Bank loan sanction letter
- Land documents (if applicable)
- GST registration certificate

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Applications must be submitted online through the Karnataka Udyog Mitra portal (www.kum.karnataka.gov.in) before 30th June 2024.

SPECIAL PROVISIONS:
- Women entrepreneurs: 100% registration fee waiver
- SC/ST entrepreneurs: Additional 5% capital subsidy
- North Karnataka units: Additional 10% incentives
- Export-oriented units: Additional benefits as per Export Policy

CONTACT:
For queries, contact District Industries Centre or call helpline: 1800-425-1000

This order is effective from 1st March 2024.
