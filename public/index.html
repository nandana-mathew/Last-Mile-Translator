GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
Circular No. 4.2.1/2024

Subject: Amendment to subsidy eligibility criteria for marginal farmers under the Agricultural Development Scheme

This circular supersedes clause 4.2 of notification dated 15.03.2023 regarding subsidy eligibility criteria. All marginal farmers owning less than 2 hectares of land and having annual income below Rs. 2,00,000 shall be eligible for subsidy under scheme XYZ. Applications must be submitted before 31st March 2026 to the concerned Taluka Agricultural Officer along with land records and income certificate.

✨ Our Translation

Plain Language Summary

If you are a small farmer owning less than 2 hectares of land and your yearly income is below Rs. 2 lakhs, you can now apply for a government subsidy. You need to submit your application before March 31, 2026, to your local agricultural office with your land documents and income proof.

What Changed?

This replaces the old rules from March 2023. The main change is in who can apply for the subsidy.

What You Need to Do

  • Check if you own less than 2 hectares of land
  • Verify your annual income is below Rs. 2,00,000
  • Collect your land records and income certificate
  • Submit application to Taluka Agricultural Officer before March 31, 2026
